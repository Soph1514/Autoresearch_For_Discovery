Given weights : List Nat and capacity : Nat, construct a binary selection list
chosen : List Nat of the same length as weights. Each chosen entry is 0 or 1.
The selected total is the sum of the pointwise products of weights and chosen.
The selection is feasible when that total is at most capacity. Maximize the
selected total over all feasible binary selection lists. Empty lists are allowed.

The instance parameters are named weights and capacity. The candidate is chosen.
