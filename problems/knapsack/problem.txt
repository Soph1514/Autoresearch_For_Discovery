Given a finite collection of items, each with a natural-number weight and value,
and a natural-number capacity, construct a subset of the items whose total weight
does not exceed the capacity and whose total value is maximum among all feasible
subsets. Each item may be selected at most once. The empty subset is allowed.

Implement solve(capacity: int, weights: list[int], values: list[int]) -> list[int].
Return distinct item indices. Weights and values are parallel nonempty lists of
nonnegative integers; zero weights and zero capacity are allowed. Maximize
total_value, the sum of the selected values. A feasible subset is valid even if
it is not optimal. Across evaluation cases maximize the sum of total values.
