Solve this 0/1 knapsack instance:

| Item | Weight | Value |
|------|--------|-------|
| 0    | 2      | 3     |
| 1    | 3      | 4     |
| 2    | 4      | 8     |
| 3    | 5      | 8     |
| 4    | 9      | 10    |

The capacity is 10. Construct a subset of the five items whose total weight does
not exceed 10 and whose total value is maximum among all feasible subsets. Each
item may be selected at most once. The required output is the selected set of
item indices. The empty subset is allowed.
